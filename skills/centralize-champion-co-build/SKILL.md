# Champion co-build

Use during a call with a mapped champion. This is a person-led sales motion.

## Run the play

Review the current map, known buying roles, and open questions before the call. Share the map through the team’s normal collaboration flow and ask the champion who is missing, who owns budget, who influences the decision, and which team runs the evaluation. Distinguish the champion’s confirmed answers from inferred reporting lines. Return the proposed map corrections, unanswered MEDDPICC questions, and next contacts. Apply chart or role changes only within the user’s requested scope.

## Example prompt

Prepare a map review with my champion at [Account]. Give me questions about budget, missing stakeholders, decision power, and evaluation ownership.

Use current Centralize tool schemas and returned IDs. Cite sources and mark uncertainty. Draft outreach and tasks unless requested.
