# Whitespace

Use when a customer’s map shows teams that may not use your product. Manual expansion motion.

## Run the play

Review the customer map and suggested contacts. Overlay customer-provided usage or adoption data to identify covered and uncovered teams; the map alone does not establish product usage. If usage data is missing, label teams as candidates and ask for that evidence. Identify the next team lead and a credible connection through the current champion. Return the team, adoption evidence or gap, target, champion, and draft intro. No customer-account expansion workflow runs automatically.

## Example prompt

Compare [Customer]’s map with our usage data. Find uncovered teams and propose champion-sponsored intros to the next team leads.

Use current Centralize tool schemas and returned IDs. Cite sources and mark uncertainty. Draft outreach and tasks unless requested.
