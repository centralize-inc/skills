# Enrichment consolidation

Use when evaluating overlapping phone and email enrichment subscriptions.

## Run the play

Review a representative sample and compare current Centralize results with the existing provider. Measure phone and email hit rates, freshness, usable results, and overlap on the same contacts. Centralize enrichment runs in the background; contact search or suggested-contact tools may also start background enrichment and need write access. Return the measured coverage, gaps, and a recommendation. Compare hit rates before claiming parity or recommending a lower ZoomInfo or LeadIQ tier; do not cancel a subscription from this workflow.

## Example prompt

Compare Centralize enrichment with our current provider on the same contact sample. Show phone and email hit rates, freshness, gaps, and evidence for consolidation.

Use current Centralize tool schemas and returned IDs. Cite sources and mark uncertainty. Draft outreach and tasks unless requested.
